ARGENTINA • #10

Lionel Messi

Forward • Playmaker • Captain

Lionel Messi is widely regarded as one of the greatest footballers in history. His game combines close control, creativity, passing, vision and exceptional finishing.

Messi became the centerpiece of Argentina's national team for many years. His international career reached a historic peak when Argentina won the 2022 FIFA World Cup.

His club career has included Barcelona, Paris Saint-Germain and Inter Miami, where he continued to influence games through both goals and assists.

PORTUGAL • #7

Cristiano Ronaldo

Forward • Goalscorer • Captain

Cristiano Ronaldo is one of the most prolific goalscorers football has ever produced. His career has been defined by extraordinary athleticism, determination and consistency.

Ronaldo developed from a talented winger into an elite goalscoring forward. His career includes major spells with Manchester United, Real Madrid, Juventus and Al-Nassr.

With Portugal, Ronaldo became a defining figure in the country's football history and helped Portugal win UEFA Euro 2016.

FRANCE • #10

Kylian Mbappé

Forward • Winger • Attacker

Kylian Mbappé emerged as one of football's most exciting young stars with his explosive acceleration, direct attacking style and ability to score important goals.

He played a major role in France's 2018 FIFA World Cup victory while still a teenager. Four years later, he became one of the central figures of the 2022 World Cup final.

His combination of speed, technical ability and finishing makes him one of the defining attackers of his generation.

NORWAY • #9

Erling Haaland

Centre Forward • Striker

Erling Haaland represents a modern generation of elite strikers. He combines physical power, speed and outstanding penalty-box instincts.

Haaland became internationally famous during his rise through European football and established himself as one of the most feared goalscorers in the modern game.

His movement without the ball is particularly dangerous. He constantly looks for spaces between defenders and attacks crosses and through balls with exceptional timing.

Modern Football Timeline

Some important moments from the careers of these stars.

2004 — Messi Arrives

Lionel Messi made his competitive first-team appearance for Barcelona and began a career that would redefine attacking football.

2008 — Ronaldo's Breakthrough

Cristiano Ronaldo won his first Ballon d'Or after an outstanding season with Manchester United.

2018 — Mbappé Becomes a World Champion

Mbappé helped France win the FIFA World Cup and became one of the youngest stars to make a major impact on the tournament.

2022 — Messi Wins the World Cup

Argentina defeated France in a dramatic final, giving Messi the biggest international trophy of his career.

2022 — Mbappé Scores a Historic Final

Mbappé scored a hat-trick in the World Cup final, becoming one of the most memorable performers in World Cup final history.

2023 — Haaland's Historic Season

Haaland helped Manchester City win the Premier League, FA Cup and Champions League in the same season.
